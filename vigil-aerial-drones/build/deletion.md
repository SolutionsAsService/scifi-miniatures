
### Step 2f. Deletion review
Every feature was asked "what is lost if this goes?"; the residual is what stayed.

| Feature | Earns its place because | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Belfry arm ridges (r 0.28 capsules) | repeat the keel crest on all six arms: the Vigil spine read from the top | KEEP |
| Belfry side battery-hatch frames | the only detail on the hull flanks (the side view is otherwise a blank slab) | KEEP |
| Belfry lower-prop 60 deg offset | the coaxial pair reads as two props, not one thick one | KEEP |
| Launch-pad ring on the flight bases (0.15 mm proud) | glue dam round the raked socket (keeps CA off the deck) | KEEP |
| Angelus boom spine (0.6 mm ridge) | carries the keel crest past the mast to the fin: one continuous Vigil line | KEEP |
| Angelus panel rings + hatch frame | without them the pod is a featureless egg at 1:56 (R1 Shklovsky) | KEEP |
| Knell outer panel joint lines | duplicate the elevon ends visually; removing them saves nothing measurable (engravings 0.25 mm deep) | KEEP (inert either way; kept for the KUB-quality "panel lines" the brief asks for) |
| Knell blisters | the brief's "access hatches / panel" quality and scale cue | KEEP |
| Launcher split-chevron mark on the front stop | the only Vigil mark on the launcher | KEEP |
| Tripod spreader bars | stiffen 14 mm legs (1.4 mm) during support removal | KEEP |
| Sized-out features: Angelus pitch links | 0.6 mm rods 0.3 mm from the mast would fail gap_check; replaced by a head block | DELETED (pre-build, recorded) |
| VL-10 as a separate default mast vs integrated | the mast socket would be empty otherwise; integrated would kill the loadout swap | KEEP separate |
Residual: no feature was removed after measurement; one (the Angelus pitch links) was removed at authoring time for a measured reason (gap rule). The deletion review found nothing that fails "what is lost".
